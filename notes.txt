Otters are known for holding hands while sleeping so they don't drift apart from each other in the water. They are one of the few animals that use tools, often using rocks to crack open shellfish.

Octopuses have three hearts and blue blood. Two hearts pump blood to the gills, while the third pumps it to the rest of the body. They are also highly intelligent and capable of solving puzzles and escaping enclosures.

Bees communicate the location of food sources to other bees through a special movement called the waggle dance. A single bee colony can contain tens of thousands of individuals working together.

The Great Wall of China is not a single continuous wall but a series of walls and fortifications built over centuries by different Chinese dynasties, primarily to protect against invasions from the north.

Python was created by Guido van Rossum and first released in 1991. It was named after the British comedy group Monty Python, not the snake.
